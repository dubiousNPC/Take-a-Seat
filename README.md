# Take a Seat

Sit on furniture in OpenMW. Look at a seat and press Activate to sit; press Activate again to stand.

## Requirements
**REQUIRES AnimRefresh_V6**

OpenMW 0.49+. `scripts/SharedRay/SharedRay_v2.lua` and `scripts/AnimRefresh/AnimRefresh_v6.lua` are bundled shared services and version-guard themselves.

Animations: the `xdbs*` sitting and transition clips, installed for all six skeleton folders (`xbase_anim`, `.1st`, `_female`, `_female.1st`, `kna`, `kna.1st`). `tools/check_anims.py` reports what each folder declares and whether every group the scripts play is reachable there.

## Settings
**Camera offset** while seated, separately for first and third person. Uses the SuperSlider renderer (v6+) when installed, plain number fields otherwise.

**Sitting**: whether to hold the seat with collision off (default on — your collision stays standing height while seated, so at a table the engine pushes you up onto it), and whether to glide out of the seat when you stand (default on — the stand-up animation does not move you, so without it you snap).

## For other mods
`scripts/take_a_seat/sitAnim_shared.lua` is data only and safe to require and hold:

```lua
local seats = require('scripts.take_a_seat.sitAnim_shared')
local kind  = seats.getSeatType(obj.recordId)   -- "bench" | nil
local group = kind and seats.SEAT_ANIM[kind]
```

Public events sent to the player: `TakeASeat_Seated { furniture, seatType, animGroup }` and `TakeASeat_Stood {}`.

## Adding seats
- A record from a plugin that may not be installed goes in `MOD_SEAT_DATABASE` under that plugin's filename. It is merged only when the plugin is loaded.
- A vanilla record goes in `BASE_SEATS`.
- Anything unlisted falls back to `SEAT_TYPE_PATTERNS`.
- Exact record IDs beat patterns. Record names lie: three vanilla records pose as a different type than their names suggest.
- `furniture_profiles.lua` is generated by `tools/gen_furniture_profiles.py`; don't edit it by hand. Hand-listed records win over generated ones.
- `solver_profiles.lua` is generated by `tools/gen_solver_profiles.py` from `data_solver_profiles/solver-profiles.json`; don't edit it by hand either. It is keyed by **model path**, not record id, so it covers furniture from plugins this mod has never heard of as long as the mesh is one the pack solved.

## Design notes
- **Authored placement first, rays second.** If the furniture's model is in the solver pack, the seat point and facing come from the pack and no ray is cast for position at all:
  `worldRoot = objectPosition + objectRotation * (slot.root * scale)`, `worldYaw = objectYaw + rad(slot.yaw)`, scale clamped to 0.25–4.0. The root is a **solved actor root**, not a seat surface, so no pivot or height offset is applied on top of it, and the yaw is used as authored — the global script is told not to add its usual half turn. `tools/test_solver_profiles.lua` pins all of that against the real pack numbers.
- **Facing.** A slot is `fixed` (no rays), `two_sided` (authored side or its opposite) or `free` (eight 45° steps). For the latter two the open-space test runs as **one** batched set of rays from the seat anchor, and the root is rebuilt for the chosen facing from `anchor` + `poseDelta`.
- **Resolve is fully async.** Everything else is a chain of async ray round-trips: push the chair clear of walls, find the seat plane, nudge the player clear. A token invalidates any chain overtaken by a new Activate, and a 3s timeout aborts one that never finishes.
- **Seat height, fallback path only.** Calibrated records (`SIT_PIVOT_OFFSET`) skip the rays entirely. Otherwise nine downward columns are clustered and the most-hit plane wins, with a fallback offset when nothing is found.
- **A sit is refused rather than coped with.** Before the teleport, while the player is still standing: a knocked-over seat (local up tilted past ~45°) is refused, and so is a seat with no validated way back out. `seat_safety.lua` does the checking with synchronous physics rays and one navmesh query, so the gate adds no async round-trip.
- **Standing up glides to a point that was checked first.** The stand-up point is the position the player was standing in when they sat, re-validated at the moment they get up; failing that, a point forward of the seated facing; failing that, the seat itself. Nothing validates — nothing moves. The move starts *with* the stand-up clip, which carries no root motion and so cannot fight it, and lasts 0.35s; with collision on, the straight line is ray-checked first and a blocked line falls back to a snap.
- **Collision comes off while seated, by default.** The player's collision capsule keeps standing height while sitting, so at a table it overlaps the table top and the engine resolves that by pushing the player up onto it. Turning collision off removes the cause; it is restored when you stand (after the glide lands), on loading a save made while seated, and never claimed if you turned it off yourself. With the setting off, a 4 Hz keeper re-asserts the seat point instead whenever the player has drifted more than 6 units — usually zero teleports, nothing per frame.
- **Movement input stands you up.** The movement override stays on for the whole sit, so a seated player cannot slide out of the chair, and the first movement press stands them up instead of being swallowed. The action names are taken from `input.actions`, the engine's own registry, and what got bound is printed once at load.
- **The chair is moved while seated and restored on stand.** A save made while seated stores the original placement, and loading restores it, so a chair can't drift between saves.
- **One priority profile per target kind** (`buildAnimProfiles`): idle, enter and exit. Every play and replay of the seated pose uses the idle profile.
- **The pose is re-issued only when lost.** The AnimRefresh subscription passes no `verify`, and the refresh checks `animation.isPlaying` first: replaying a looping pose restarts it from frame 0.
- **Enter and exit one-shots** have a 1.5s backstop timer, because a missing clip never sends its ended event; the longest shipped transition measures 1.033s.
- **A group the skeleton lacks resolves to one it has.** `GROUP_FALLBACKS` maps the RA-set transitions onto the `tassit*` clips, which is how a beast-skeleton player gets a sit-down at all.
- **Beds are off** (`BEDS_ENABLED = false`) until the sleeping clips are confirmed in every skeleton set.
- Sitting restores fatigue up to its fortified maximum and never lowers it.
